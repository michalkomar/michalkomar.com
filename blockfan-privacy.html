BlockFan Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

BlockFan is designed to be private. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data and accounts

BlockFan has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or third-party SDKs. Gameplay progress and preferences are stored only on your device using Apple’s local storage.

Network access

Gameplay works completely offline. BlockFan does not operate a server and does not send gameplay data to the developer.

Children

Because BlockFan collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored BlockFan data by deleting the app. Reinstalling starts with a fresh local game unless your operating system restores app data from a device backup.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the BlockFan support form.
